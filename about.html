ABOUT CHECKERDISCOUNT

Helping You Shop Smarter

CheckerDiscount was created to make online deal discovery simpler, clearer and more useful for everyday shoppers.

Our Mission

Online shopping can be confusing. The same product may be available from different retailers at different prices.

CheckerDiscount aims to help shoppers discover attractive offers, understand potential savings and check the retailer's deal before making a purchase.

What We Do

We curate and display selected deals from retailers and provide useful information to help shoppers make better purchasing decisions.

  • Discover selected deals and offers.
  • Show reference prices and deal prices where available.
  • Calculate potential savings.
  • Provide links to retailer offers.
  • Make deal information easier to understand.

Our Approach

We believe a good deal website should be simple and transparent. We do not want to overwhelm shoppers with unnecessary information.

Our goal is to help users quickly understand what an offer is, how much they could potentially save, and where they can check the retailer's current offer.

Affiliate Relationships

Some links on CheckerDiscount may be affiliate links. This means we may receive a commission when a qualifying purchase is made through certain links.

Affiliate relationships do not change the price you pay at the retailer.
