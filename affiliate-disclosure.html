Affiliate Disclosure

We believe in being clear about how CheckerDiscount may earn money when you use certain links on our website.

Our Affiliate Relationship

CheckerDiscount may participate in affiliate marketing programs. This means that some links on our website may be affiliate links.

If you click an affiliate link and make a qualifying purchase, we may receive a commission from the retailer or affiliate network, at no additional cost to you.

You do not pay extra because of an affiliate link.

How Affiliate Links Work

When you click a "Check This Deal" or similar shopping link on CheckerDiscount, you may be redirected to the retailer's website.

If that link is an affiliate link and you complete a qualifying purchase, the retailer or affiliate network may provide us with a commission.

The purchase itself is completed directly with the retailer. CheckerDiscount is not the seller of the products listed on external retailer websites.

Our Deal Recommendations

Our goal is to help users discover and compare attractive deals from online stores.

We aim to present deal information clearly and honestly. However, prices, availability, shipping costs, promotions and other details can change at any time on the retailer's website.

Always check the final price, availability and purchase terms on the retailer's website before completing an order.

Affiliate Links Do Not Increase Your Price

Using an affiliate link does not normally add an additional charge to your purchase price.

The retailer determines the final price and purchase terms. Any commission received by CheckerDiscount comes from the applicable affiliate program or retailer relationship.

Retailers and Affiliate Programs

CheckerDiscount may work with different retailers, affiliate networks or shopping programs as the website grows.

Affiliate relationships may change over time. The presence of a product or store on CheckerDiscount does not mean that the retailer endorses CheckerDiscount.

Why We Use Affiliate Links

Affiliate commissions can help support the operation, maintenance and future development of CheckerDiscount.

This may allow us to continue providing deal discovery, comparison and savings tools to users.

Your Choice

You are never required to use an affiliate link. You are free to visit a retailer directly if you prefer.

We appreciate users who choose to support CheckerDiscount through our affiliate links.
